# LendMIT-frontend

## About LendMIT

LendMIT is a peer-to-peer lending and borrowing platform designed for the MIT community. It allows users to share resources they already have and borrow items they temporarily need, from baking supplies to clothing to dorm essentials.

Users can:
- Create an account and verify their email.
- Post items they’re willing to lend or requests for items they want to borrow.
- Browse listings posted by others and connect through email.
- Manage their listings’ availability and intent (lend or borrow) directly from the interface.
